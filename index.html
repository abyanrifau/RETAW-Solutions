FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does RETAW Solutions do?

RETAW Solutions sells and installs RO (reverse osmosis) water purifiers for homes and businesses in the Maldives. Its main product, the S1 RO Water Purifier, cleans ordinary tap water and delivers it through a dedicated faucet, so households don't need to buy bottled water.

How much does the RETAW S1 water purifier cost?

The RETAW S1 RO Water Purifier costs MVR 5,660. Installation is free.

How much does a filter change cost, and how often is it needed?

A filter change for the S1 costs MVR 600 and is needed every 6 months, which comes to MVR 1,200 a year. Regular filter changes are required: without them, the S1 can't clean water properly.

What warranty and support does RETAW Solutions provide?

The S1 comes with a 2-year warranty, free installation and 4-hour fault rectification from the RETAW Solutions team.

How does the S1 purify water?

The S1 filters water in two stages. First, a patented four-layer PCT Composite Filter Element (non-woven fabric, carbon fiber, high precision PP cotton and a rear carbon rod) removes sand, rust, chlorine, odour and fine particles. Then a 400 GPD DOW RO membrane, with filtration precision down to 0.0001 micron, removes heavy metals, bacteria, viruses, scale and organic matter.

How much clean water can the S1 produce?

The S1 can purify up to 1,500 litres of water a day, about 1 litre per minute.

How much can a household save compared with bottled water?

RETAW Solutions estimates that one person spends about MVR 420 a month on bottled drinking water (90 litres, or 60 bottles of 1.5 L at about MVR 7 each). For a 3-person household spending MVR 1,260 a month, the S1 pays for itself in about 5 months and saves about MVR 63,940 over 5 years, after the purifier price and filter changes. The savings calculator works this out for any household.

Yes. The S1 RO Water Purifier is designed for both households and businesses.
