How it works

From your tap to your glass in two stages

Every drop passes through RETAW's patented PCT composite element, then a DOW reverse osmosis membrane, before it reaches your faucet.

  1. 1

    PCT Composite Filter Element

    Four layers in one cartridge remove sand, rust, chlorine, odour and fine particles.

  2. 2

    400 GPD RO Membrane

    Filters down to 0.0001 micron, removing heavy metals, bacteria, viruses and scale.

  3. Clean water at your faucet

    Around 1 litre a minute, whenever you need it.
