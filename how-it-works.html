How it works

What happens between your tap and your glass

The S1 cleans your water in two stages: RETAW's patented PCT composite filter element, then a DOW reverse osmosis membrane. Here's what each one does.

  1. Tap water in

    Your existing water supply feeds the unit.

  2. 1

    PCT composite element

    Four filtration layers in a single cartridge.

  3. 2

    400 GPD RO membrane

    Filtration precision down to 0.0001 micron.

  4. Clean water out

    Around 1 L/min from your dedicated faucet.

Stage 1

The PCT Composite Filter Element

Four filtration layers work together in one cartridge. The non-woven fabric and PP cotton catch particles, and the carbon layers target chlorine, odour and taste.

  1. LAYER 01

    Non-woven fabric

    The outer layer and first line of defence. It filters out sand, rust and other visible particles before the water goes deeper into the element.

    • Sand
    • Rust
    • Visible particles
  2. LAYER 02

    Carbon fiber

    Absorbs residual chlorine and odour, so the water already tastes better before it reaches the finer stages.

    • Residual chlorine
    • Odour
  3. LAYER 03

    High precision PP cotton

    A fine polypropylene layer that filters colloids, suspended solids and other fine particles the first layer can't catch.

    • Colloids
    • Suspended solids
    • Fine particles
  4. LAYER 04

    Rear carbon rod

    A second round of odour absorption at the end of the element, polishing the water for a cleaner, fresher taste.

    • Remaining odours
    • Improves taste
    The PCT element must be changed every 6 months (MVR 600 per filter change). Without regular filter changes, the S1 can't clean water properly.

Stage 2

400 GPD DOW RO Membrane

Next, the water is pushed through a reverse osmosis membrane with a filtration precision of up to 0.0001 micron, fine enough to hold back what the first stage can't.

  • Removes heavy metals
  • Removes bacteria and viruses
  • Removes scale and organic matter
0.0001 µmfiltration precision
2 yearsmembrane lifespan

The result

Clean water, straight from your own faucet

After both stages, purified water flows to a dedicated faucet at around 1 litre per minute, up to 1500 litres a day. No bottles, no deliveries, no running out.
